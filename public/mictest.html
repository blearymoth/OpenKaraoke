Mic latency test

Can this PC run the microphones through software effects (reverb, echo) without singers hearing themselves late? This page measures the real delay from the mic, through the browser, out of your speakers and back. Open it on the PC that will play the party, with the speakers you will use.

Setup

The browser asks for the microphone the first time. Pick the mic and speakers you'll use at the party; Bluetooth and TV speakers add a lot of delay.

1 Measure the delay

Turn the speakers to a normal party volume, hold the mic about 30 cm from a speaker and keep the room quiet. The test plays six short clicks and listens for each one.

– ms round trip

2 Hear yourself

Sends the mic straight to the speakers, the way live effects would. Sing a few lines and judge the delay by ear. Keep the mic away from the speakers, or use headphones: a mic pointed at a speaker howls.

Effect

“Extra delay” adds delay on purpose, so you can feel how much a singer tolerates before it gets distracting.

What the browser reports

The browser's own estimate only covers its buffers. The measured round trip also includes drivers, HDMI/TV sound processing, Bluetooth and the distance between mic and speaker (about 3 ms per metre).
